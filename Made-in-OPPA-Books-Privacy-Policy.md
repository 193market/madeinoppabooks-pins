# Privacy Policy - Made in OPPA Books publishing tool

Last updated: 2026-10-09

This tool is a private publishing script operated by Made in OPPA Books. It is not offered to other users.

- **What it does:** it posts our own promotional images and text (printable puzzle books, worksheets and cookbooks sold on Etsy and Payhip) to our own Pinterest and Threads business accounts.
- **What it collects:** nothing about visitors or other Pinterest users. It does not read other people's pins, boards, profiles, followers or messages.
- **Data stored:** access tokens for our own accounts, kept on our own computer and never shared or sold.
- **Third parties:** we do not share any data with third parties.
- **Contact:** open an issue in this repository.
- **Artwork:** images are created with the help of AI tools and are labeled as such.
